Frequently asked questions

The answers you're looking for.

Straightforward answers to common questions about Malaysia My Second Home.

What is Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H)?

MM2H is a Malaysian government long-stay programme for qualifying foreign nationals. It has different categories and requirements. It is not the same as permanent residence or citizenship.

Can I include my family members?

Eligible dependents may include a spouse, qualifying children, parents or parents-in-law under the applicable category and official rules. Some dependent conditions vary by age and circumstances.

Do I need to buy property?

The main MM2H categories have compulsory residential property purchase requirements. The minimum price depends on the category. SEZ/SFZ property rules are specific to qualifying developments.

Do I need to stay in Malaysia every year?

Published requirements generally specify 90 cumulative days annually for participants aged 25–49, with eligible family members able to fulfil the requirement. Requirements for participants aged 50+ differ.

Is the fixed deposit the same as the application fee?

No. The fixed deposit is a financial requirement held with an eligible Malaysian financial institution. Participating, processing, service, immigration, medical and property-related costs are distinct and may also apply.

How do I submit an official MM2H application?

New applications must be submitted through a business licensed for MM2H by the Malaysian Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture. Confirm an agency's current licence status before proceeding.
